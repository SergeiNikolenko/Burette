Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Burrete: Local-first molecular file inspection
for macOS, Quick Look, and agent workflows” as an Original Software Publication
in SoftwareX.

Burrete is open-source research software for the recurring inspection step
between generating a molecular artifact and starting heavier scientific
analysis. It combines Finder Quick Look, a Tauri desktop workspace, a
source-built iPhone preview target, explicit routing to established molecular
components, and typed agent interfaces over bounded workspace state. The
article is deliberately framed as a software-integration contribution: it does
not claim a new molecular-graphics, cheminformatics, docking, simulation, or
AI drug-design algorithm.

The manuscript follows the current SoftwareX LaTeX template and includes the
five required sections, a mandatory code-metadata table, four figures, and
fewer than 3,000 words. Burrete version 1.0.22 is publicly available on GitHub
under the MIT License, with documentation, sample files, source code, and macOS
release artifacts:

https://github.com/SergeiNikolenko/Burrete/tree/softwarex-v1.0.22

The work has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. I am
the sole author and approve the submitted version. I am also the primary
developer of Burrete and may benefit reputationally from its adoption; no other
competing interests are declared. The manuscript transparently reports that
the project is at an early adoption stage and does not claim independent
publication impact that has not yet been demonstrated.

Thank you for considering this software article.
