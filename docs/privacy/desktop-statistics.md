# Burette desktop usage statistics

Publication status: draft for the configured collector. Before publication,
add the actual hosting provider, storage location, recipient/subprocessor and
international transfer information. Do not enable collection in a release with
an incomplete notice.

## Your choice

Sharing is off until you choose “Share statistics” or enable it in
Settings → General after reading the disclosure. Refusing changes no features.
You can turn it off in the same settings at any time. Your choice applies to
this installation; we do not track you across devices.

## What is sent

After consent, Burette creates a random installation identifier. Statistics
include activity days, broad feature-view categories, broad error categories,
app version and CPU architecture (Apple Silicon or Intel). Batches include a
random identifier to avoid counting retries twice and the consent-notice
version. The service records the receipt day and initial consent receipt time.

We do not send your documents, molecules, filenames, paths, typed text, raw
error messages, crash dumps or local diagnostic logs. We do not collect your
name, email, account details or hardware identifiers through these statistics.
The service necessarily receives your network address to deliver HTTPS; it
does not add it to statistics or retain it in request logs under this service's
configuration.

The random installation identifier links events from one installation. This
is pseudonymous data, not a promise of complete anonymity. We do not link it
to landing-page analytics or use it for advertising, profiling individuals or
automated decisions about you.

## Storage and deletion

Event records expire within 90 days. Consent records remain while the
installation has event records. Retry identifiers expire within two days.
The database stores a cryptographic digest of the installation identifier,
not the identifier itself. Access is restricted to operating and improving
Burette; statistics are not sold.

Turning sharing off stops collection, discards unsent events and requests
deletion of the installation's shared records. If offline, Burette shows
“Deletion is pending” and retries while the app is running. Reenabling waits
for deletion to finish and creates a new identifier. A digest-only deletion
marker remains for 90 days to reject late uploads; it contains no activity.

Uninstalling before deletion completes may remove the local identifier needed
to identify your records. Automatic retention still applies.

## Access and questions

While sharing is enabled, “Export my statistics” in Settings → General saves
the records associated with this installation. Turn sharing off to withdraw
consent and request erasure. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took
place before withdrawal.

Contact the email above for questions or requests concerning access,
correction, erasure, restriction or other rights available under applicable
law. Because statistics are not linked to email accounts, your email alone
does not identify your records; you can use the in-app controls without
providing identifying documents. Depending on applicable law, you may also
lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.
